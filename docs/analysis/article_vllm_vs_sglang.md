# Article-implied vLLM-vs-SGLang advantage (Backboard cancelled out)

Derived from the article's two 13-metric ratio tables: (Backboard/SGLang) ÷ (Backboard/vLLM) = vLLM advantage
(>1 = vLLM better; latency ratios are SGLang/vLLM). Published ratios have 2 decimals ⇒ each value is ±~0.5–1 %.
These are **targets for our measured matched-load comparison** (`g4bench analyze` → engine_comparison.csv),
independent of Backboard's proprietary engine. Not measurements. Generated by `g4bench.engine_compare.article_implied`.

Observations: vLLM leads SGLang 1.4–2.1× on throughput/TPOT at every point in the article, but TTFT tails are near parity
(p99 TTFT 0.97–1.06 at 8K), i.e. the article's SGLang gap is a decode-speed gap, not a prefill gap. With speculative
decoding dominating decode speed (roofline), the acceptance length each engine achieves is the first thing to measure in E1.
